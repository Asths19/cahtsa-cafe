✦ A Little Bit of Magic and a Little Bit of Love.... ✦

At Cahtsa's Crumbs, we believe the best stories aren't written—they are baked. From the first dusting of flour to the final pinch of magic, every pastry is a tiny invitation to slow down, take a seat in your favourite corner, and stay a while. Welcome to a world made of butter, dreams, and just a few stray crumbs.

We bake everything fresh each morning — from cloud-soft croissants to mismatched cakes with too much frosting ,as it should be. Whether you're here for a solo afternoon or a long chat with someone dear, there's always a seat and a treat waiting for you.

Come as you are. Stay for the crumbs. 🍂
